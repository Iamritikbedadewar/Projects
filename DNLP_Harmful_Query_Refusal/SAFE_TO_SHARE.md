# Sharing Checklist

The repository's generic source code, empty templates, and public references are
designed to be shareable. The following items are **not** safe to share:

- the `matplus-data` folder or any file copied from it;
- `private_outputs/`;
- fine-tuned checkpoints or tokenizer files;
- raw or lightly redacted predictions;
- screenshots of data, console examples, or local error analysis;
- prompts containing confidential examples;
- synthetic examples produced from or closely paraphrasing confidential examples;
- aggregate results unless the agreement permits derived results.
